Help & support

Plan and track

Use the + button to plan or log an activity. On Today, choose What are you doing now? to start a timer. You can run several timers, pause and resume them, or finish them to keep the recorded time. Saved activities and recent activities make repeated tasks quicker to start.

Read your timer

A minus sign means time remains until the planned end. A plus sign means the timer has passed that end. An unsigned timer counts tracked time for an activity without a planned end. The smaller timer shows total tracked time, excluding pauses.

Apple Watch

Dayline on Apple Watch needs the app on its paired iPhone. Install it from the Watch app, open Dayline on both devices, and use Apple Watch → Sync now from the iPhone options menu. A disconnected Watch can queue a change until the iPhone confirms it. On the Watch time editor, tap Hour or Minute and turn the Digital Crown. Crossing midnight changes the day; future times are unavailable.

Live Activities and widgets

If a timer is missing from the Lock Screen, open Dayline, then Today → Edit timer → Restore Live Activity. If the app offers Allow Live Activities in Settings, enable them there. Widgets and Watch updates follow the system’s refresh limits and may take time to update.

Apple Health

Enable workout or sleep imports in Settings and choose the matching read permissions. Dayline imports movement and rest to help you understand your routine. If nothing appears, check the permissions and original records in Apple Health, then sync from Apple Health details. Apple does not tell Dayline whether you denied read access. Dayline never writes to Health.

Automatic tracking

Enable Automatically track scheduled events and choose the categories you want. Scheduled time is reconciled when Dayline next runs; iOS does not promise to launch the app or a Live Activity at every scheduled boundary. Notifications are scheduled separately. Auto-complete planned activities is a separate option and starts off.

Shortcuts

Use Settings → Shortcuts & automations for Dayline’s actions. For an automation that finishes a timer by name, give Start Tracking Activity and Finish Timers by Name the same activity name. A blank start name uses the category name. Test the automation with your phone both unlocked and locked.

Your history

The activity database and Watch/widget caches stay local and are excluded from automatic device backups because they may contain Health data. Export activities saves a JSON copy of your plans and tracked activities for your records. Imported Apple Health records are excluded; they remain in Apple Health. Dayline cannot currently import this file. Deleting or replacing the app can remove your only editable copy. Use the privacy policy for permission and deletion controls.
