<h1>Privacy policy</h1><p class="date">Effective November 12, 2026</p><section><h2>Your data stays with you</h2><p>Dayline is a personal planner and time tracker. Your plans, activity names, categories, saved activities, timer records, and settings are stored on your device. Dayline does not run a server for your activity data, require an account, include advertising or analytics SDKs, or track you across apps and websites. The developer does not receive your activity or Health data through the app.</p></section><section><h2>Apple Health</h2><p>If you enable workout or sleep imports and grant permission, Dayline reads workout types, workout start and end times, sleep intervals, record identifiers, and source app names from Apple Health. It uses these records to show movement and rest alongside your day and to calculate your activity totals without counting overlapping manual time twice. Health access is optional. Dayline does not write to Apple Health, use Health data for advertising, sell it, or send it to a developer-operated service.</p></section><section><h2>Your Watch, widgets, and notifications</h2><p>Dayline sends your schedule, timer state, saved activity choices, and daily totals to your paired Apple Watch using Apple’s Watch Connectivity. Totals can include imported Health time. Widgets share a small local snapshot with Dayline; Live Activities and notifications can display activity names and times on your Lock Screen. You control their visibility in system settings. Dayline uses local notifications, not a developer-operated push service.</p></section><section><h2>Storage and backups</h2><p>Dayline does not provide iCloud or cross-device database sync. Because the activity database and Watch/widget caches can contain Health information, Dayline excludes them from automatic device backups. Your activity history therefore is not restored by an ordinary device-backup restore. General app preferences may still be backed up by the operating system. Keep any exports you need before deleting Dayline or replacing your device. Dayline currently exports JSON for your own records but cannot import or restore those files.</p></section><section><h2>Exports you choose</h2><p>Export activities creates a readable JSON file containing your plans, manually logged and timed activities, categories, repeat schedules, and saved activities. Imported Apple Health records are excluded. Dayline asks before opening the system file exporter. The destination and any later sharing are your choice. An exported copy is outside Dayline’s control; deleting data in Dayline does not delete that copy.</p></section><section><h2>Permissions, retention, and deletion</h2><p>Your records remain in Dayline until you delete them or delete the app. Settings → Delete all activities removes plans, repeat schedules, saved activities, tracked time, and imported Health records; it keeps your categories and general settings. Settings → Apple Health details → Remove imported Health data removes the imported copies and turns off imports. Neither action changes Apple Health’s original records. Turning an import switch off stops new imports but keeps previous imports. You can also revoke Health read permission in Apple Health or system settings. Pending Watch updates are replaced when the devices next connect. To remove all local copies immediately, delete Dayline from your iPhone or iPad and your Apple Watch, and delete any exports separately.</p></section><section><h2>Support and external pages</h2><p>If you choose to contact support, the developer receives the message and any information you include. Do not send activity exports or Health records unless you intend to share them. Support correspondence is used to respond and resolve the issue and is retained only as needed for that purpose or legal obligations. You may request deletion of support correspondence through the same contact. Opening the support or privacy website uses your browser and its connection to the website host; the app does not attach your activity data.</p></section><section><h2>Changes to this policy</h2><p>If Dayline’s data practices change, this policy and its effective date will be updated. You can read this policy offline in Settings → Privacy policy. The online policy and support contact are available from the App Store listing and from Settings.</p></section>
