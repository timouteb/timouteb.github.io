When can I download Dayline?

Dayline is being prepared for the App Store. A download link will appear here when it’s available.

Do I need an Apple Watch?

No. You can plan and track on your iPhone or iPad. The optional Apple Watch companion lets you manage activities from your wrist and works with its paired iPhone.

Do I have to connect Apple Health?

Not at all. Planning, timers, and manual logging work without Health access. You can choose to import workouts or sleep and control those permissions in your device settings.

Where is my activity history stored?

Your history is stored locally. Dayline does not currently offer iCloud database sync. Its activity database is excluded from automatic device backups because it can contain Health information. You can export your plans and manually tracked activities for your records, but Dayline cannot currently restore those exports. See the privacy policy for details.
